# Second edition — a handmade little internet

## Illustration guide

Medium: intricate gouache and coloured-pencil illustration, scanned from cold-press paper. Visible pigment grain, fine graphite contours, occasional imperfect scissor-cut edges. No vector clip art, photorealism, plastic 3D, or hazy luxury styling.

Lighting: warm, broad window light from upper left. Shadows fall lower right; contact shadows are compact and coloured olive-grey. Keep shadows local to the object rather than blurring an entire composition.

Palette: linen #F4F0E6, aged ivory #FFF9EF, sage #BAC5A7, olive ink #4D604A, dusty blue #B8CDE0, navy #293F56, cranberry #944C55, ochre #C7A56C. Warm wood, scratched brass, worn paper, matte enamel, stitched cloth and bookbinding leather supply material contrast.

Perspective: near-front objects with modest visible top/right surfaces; flat overhead stationery. Use a shared upper-left light source. Illustrations must retain clear silhouettes at small sizes. Important text is always live HTML.

Composition: each scene gets a different arrangement. A compact personal-web contents rail anchors a fixed-height, responsive stage. On phones, the rail becomes a chapter selector. The scene does not scroll vertically. Reading is paginated; optional editor controls and archives open in contained views.

## Asset layers

- Desktop still life: a CRT/computer arrangement behind a separately interactive call window.
- Magazine cover artwork: image on the separately rotating front cover; title and typography remain live.
- Capsule-machine shell: printed enamel body with chamber and mechanism openings. Capsules, glass highlights, rotating lever, hatch and dispensed capsule remain independent layers.
- Passport cover: raster leather and embossed ornament on a separately rotating cover; live text and independent pages.
- Fortune vessel: detailed ceramic/wood container in front of thirteen independent moving sticks.
- Photo studio collage: static desk ephemera behind the editable, exportable photograph and draggable stickers.
- Envelope: printed stationery surfaces separated using CSS clipping into backing, flap and front; the letter remains a separate animated element.

## Motion key poses

Call: window enters from the desktop taskbar → controls depress → connection status → staggered messages. No idle bobbing.
Magazine: rest → slight cover lift → spine rotation with travelling shadow → inner spread → next scene.
Machine: lever resists → engages → capsule tumble → hatch releases → gravity arc with two diminishing impacts → split capsule and expanding keepsake.
Passport: page corner lifts → page turns about binding → new spread settles → ink stamp impact. On phone, show one readable leaf at a time.
Fortune: container preloads → individual sticks jostle at different phases → selected stick rises → extraction → paper unfurls in segments.
Photo: stock slides from printer → adhesive contact → sticker settles. Export uses the same canvas as the preview.
Letter: quiet thread draw → flap opens → sheet rises → readable page; page changes are restrained. Reduced-motion paths use immediate state changes without decorative motion.

Original personal photos and documents remain unchanged and clearly marked as pending until supplied. Generated imagery is illustration, never claimed to be historical media. No public deployment is authorized.

## Chinese typography — November 8 update
Chinese uses bundled LXGW WenKai Regular (霞鹜文楷), chosen for its warm pen-written forms and readable paragraph proportions. It is the CJK fallback in all three existing type roles; English retains Literata, DM Sans and Caveat. Magazine Chinese headings use normal tracking and a 1.25 line height; Chinese paragraphs use a 1.65 line height. The WOFF2 is served locally and embedded in the standalone build. SIL OFL license is included in FONT-LICENSES/lxgw-wenkai.txt.
